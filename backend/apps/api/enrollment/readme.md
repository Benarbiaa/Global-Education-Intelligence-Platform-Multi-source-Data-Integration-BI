# Enrollment & Graduation Analytics KPIs - Comprehensive Overview

## KPI 1: Outbound Student Mobility (Enrollment Abroad)

**Why It's Important:**

* **Global Exposure:** Measures the percentage of students studying abroad
* **Talent Distribution:** Identifies where students seek education internationally
* **Policy Evaluation:** Helps governments understand the impact of scholarships or exchange programs
* **Benchmarking:** Compares countries’ student mobility patterns

**Calculation:**

```python
Outbound_Mobility_Ratio = SUM(percentage_of_students_studying_abroad_per_country)
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Guides scholarship and mobility program design
* Assesses international competitiveness of higher education
* Supports strategic partnerships with foreign institutions

---

## KPI 2: Gross Enrollment Ratio

**Why It's Important:**

* **Access Indicator:** Measures enrollment relative to population of school-age students
* **Equity Assessment:** Highlights disparities across regions or countries
* **Policy Impact:** Evaluates effectiveness of enrollment policies
* **Trend Analysis:** Monitors growth or decline in education participation

**Calculation:**

```python
Gross_Enrollment_Ratio = (Total_Enrollment / Population_of_School_Age) * 100
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Identifies gaps in access to education
* Informs resource allocation and policy interventions
* Tracks progress toward national or global education targets

---

## KPI 3: Public vs Private Enrollment Share

**Why It's Important:**

* **System Composition:** Shows distribution of public and private education
* **Policy Assessment:** Evaluates government and private sector roles
* **Market Insight:** Supports planning for education infrastructure and investment
* **Trend Monitoring:** Tracks shifts between public and private enrollment over time

**Calculation:**

```python
Public_Share = SUM(enrollment_in_public_institutions) / SUM(total_enrollment) * 100
Private_Share = SUM(enrollment_in_private_institutions) / SUM(total_enrollment) * 100
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Informs policy on public-private partnerships
* Monitors education system balance and accessibility
* Guides investments in private or public education sectors

---

## KPI 4: Year-over-Year (YoY) Enrollment Growth

**Why It's Important:**

* **Growth Tracking:** Measures annual changes in enrollment percentages
* **Early Warning:** Detects declining enrollment trends
* **Policy Evaluation:** Assesses effectiveness of interventions
* **Comparative Analysis:** Compares growth rates across countries or institutions

**Calculation:**

```python
YoY_Growth = ((Current_Year_Avg_Enrollment - Previous_Year_Avg_Enrollment) / Previous_Year_Avg_Enrollment) * 100
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Enables proactive policy and resource decisions
* Highlights countries with accelerating or declining enrollment
* Supports benchmarking against regional or global trends

---

## KPI 5: Average Graduation Rate per Country

**Why It's Important:**

* **Completion Indicator:** Measures the success of students completing studies
* **System Effectiveness:** Reflects quality and retention in education
* **Policy Relevance:** Evaluates interventions targeting student success
* **International Benchmarking:** Compares national graduation rates globally

**Calculation:**

```python
Average_Graduation_Rate = AVG(percentage_of_graduates_per_country_per_year)
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Guides curriculum and retention improvement policies
* Identifies countries or regions with high or low success rates
* Supports planning for higher education and workforce readiness

---

## KPI 6: Graduation Rate by Gender

**Why It's Important:**

* **Equity Monitoring:** Tracks gender disparities in graduation rates
* **Policy Assessment:** Evaluates effectiveness of gender-targeted programs
* **Socioeconomic Insight:** Highlights systemic barriers for female or male students
* **Benchmarking:** Compares gender parity across institutions and countries

**Calculation:**

```python
Female_Graduation_Rate = AVG(percentage_of_female_graduates)
Male_Graduation_Rate = AVG(percentage_of_male_graduates)
```

**Strategic Value:**

* Supports gender equity initiatives in education
* Informs scholarship and mentorship programs
* Highlights areas for targeted intervention to improve parity
