# Government Education Expenditure Analytics KPIs - Comprehensive Overview

## KPI 1: Education Expenditure % of Government Budget

**Why It's Important:**

  * **National Priority Assessment:** Indicates how much of the **government budget** is allocated to education.
  * **Policy Benchmarking:** Helps evaluate education policy effectiveness.
  * **Comparative Analysis:** Compares investment levels across countries.
  * **Strategic Planning:** Guides future education funding decisions.

**Calculation:**

```python
Education_Expenditure_Percentage = AVG(expenditure_percentage_per_country_per_year)
```

**Strategic Value:**

  * Measures **commitment** to education.
  * Supports evidence-based policy making.
  * Helps identify underfunded regions or sectors.

## KPI 2: Scholarship Investment per Year

**Why It's Important:**

  * **Student Support Tracking:** Measures the total investment in **scholarships**.
  * **Talent Retention & Attraction:** Indicates support for students to pursue higher education.
  * **Policy Evaluation:** Evaluates effectiveness of scholarship programs.
  * **Resource Allocation:** Guides funding priorities for student financial aid.

**Calculation:**

```python
Total_Scholarship_Investment = SUM(scholarships_per_country_per_year)
```

**Strategic Value:**

  * Monitors scholarship trends.
  * Helps optimize student support programs.
  * Provides insight for international student mobility initiatives.

-----

## KPI 3: Tertiary Expenditure per Year

**Why It's Important:**

  * **Higher Education Funding Analysis:** Tracks total spending on **tertiary education**.
  * **Investment Trends:** Monitors growth or decline in university funding.
  * **Policy Impact:** Evaluates the effectiveness of higher education policies.
  * **Comparative Assessment:** Compares investment across countries and over time.

**Calculation:**

```python
Total_Tertiary_Expenditure = SUM(tertiary_expenditure_per_country_per_year)
```

**Strategic Value:**

  * Guides national education funding strategies.
  * Supports planning for research, infrastructure, and student support.
  * Enables benchmarking against regional and global standards.
